About Confie's

Our Story

Confie's Beddings & Curtains is a creative home décor brand focused on making bedrooms and living spaces warmer, softer and more stylish.

Our Mission

To make beautiful home textiles accessible while giving customers thoughtful choices in fabric, colour, pattern and finish.

Our Unique Technique

Room Mood Match connects three elements — bedding, curtains and accessories — using one shared colour story. Instead of buying items separately, you build a coordinated room.
